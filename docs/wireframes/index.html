UI/UX redesign: wireframes

Inka Arthouse Hub · Notion + Supabase direction · 15 screens (Order submission is not included) · Round 2, checked against the app · 24 Nov 2026

Grey, mid-fidelity wireframes. Layout, hierarchy and interaction patterns only; colours, icons and copy are placeholders. Use the sun/moon switch (or Ctrl Shift L) for light and dark mode. Every screen has a Design notes panel (bottom right) explaining the numbered pins. Press Ctrl K on any screen to see the command palette.
